Cockpit & Tactical Avionics Systems

An authentic high-fidelity environment built around the primary Civil Air Patrol workhorse: the Cessna 182T Skylane NAV III. Re-engineered with authentic Lycoming IO-540 performance, Garmin G1000 EIS instrumentation, and an observer station equipped with Becker 517 and RHOTHETA RT-600 direction finders.

Airframe Engineering

Cessna 182T Skylane NAV III (CAP 487)

X-Plane 12 natively includes only the C172SP Skyhawk. To fulfill authentic CAP mission profiles requiring mountain performance, 5.5+ hour loiter endurance, and constant-speed propeller operation, we engineered an automated package builder (scripts/build_c182t_package.py) that transforms the high-fidelity base airframe into a dedicated Skylane.

  • ✓ Powerplant: 230 HP Lycoming IO-540-AB1A5 (541 cu in, fuel-injected, 2,400 RPM redline).
  • ✓ Constant-Speed Propeller: McCauley 3-blade governor-controlled propeller (10° low / 40° high pitch).
  • ✓ Garmin G1000 EIS Auto-Integration: Setting constant-speed prop type automatically triggers the G1000 MFD to display the dual Manifold Pressure (MAN IN HG) and Tachometer (RPM) gauges with authentic C182T green/yellow/red arcs.
  • ✓ Cooling & Drag: Manual cowl flaps modeled for climb thermal management and descent airspeed control.

Fleet Standard

Becker SAR-DF 517 Station Panel

The primary electronic homing head across the Civil Air Patrol fleet. Monitored alongside the Garmin G1000 MFD and Garmin GMA 1347 audio panel.

  • ✓ Display Cutout: Transparent region (196, 136, 787×592) allows popped-out X-Plane MFD windows or Air Player video streams to show cleanly behind bezel controls.
  • ✓ Four-Band Homing: 121.500 MHz (VHF civilian), 243.000 MHz (UHF military), 156.800 MHz (Maritime CH 16), and 406.025 MHz (COSPAS-SARSAT).
  • ✓ True Six-Page DCU: Page 1 Bearing & Spread, Page 2 360° Compass Rose, Page 3 406.025 MHz emergency burst decode, Page 6 adjustable frequency tables.
Modernization Fleet

RHOTHETA RT-600 Modernization Panel

Replicates the modern multi-band Doppler DF system currently being fielded across upgraded CAP search airframes.

  • ✓ Full 19-Channel 406 Grid: Seven pre-programmed CPSAR groups covering 406.022 MHz to 406.076 MHz in 3 kHz increments.
  • ✓ Concurrent Dual-Band Scan (3.3.3): Automatically sweeps between assigned VHF civilian frequency (121.500 or 121.600 MHz) and UHF 406 MHz channels.
  • ✓ Direct Hardware Rotary Control: Tuning, squelch thresholding, and page switching operable via mouse wheel, touch drag, or physical Knobster USB hardware.

Tactical Communications

Technisonic TDFM-136B & Garmin GMA 1347 Integration

Airborne tactical communications require specialized features beyond civilian airband VHF. CAP V-SAR implements the full operational specifications of the airborne transceiver and audio management panel.

Airborne Transceiver

Technisonic TDFM-136B VHF-FM

The standard tactical FM transceiver installed across CAP search aircraft, bridging airborne crews with Wing repeaters, ground teams, and local emergency management agencies across 136–174 MHz.

  • ✓ Dedicated Guard Receiver (GD1 / GD2): Dual-receiver architecture allows continuous background monitoring of emergency guard channels (168.650 MHz / 121.500 MHz) independent of the selected main channel.
  • ✓ 60-Second Transmit Time-Out Timer (TOT): Prevents accidental stuck-microphone net lockout. Continuous PTT transmits for 60 seconds before automatically clamping RF output and sounding an aural alert.
  • ✓ 20-Channel Priority Scan Engine: Fast channel scanning with programmable priority sampling, allowing crews to monitor squadron base net, air-to-ground tactical, and flight following concurrently.
  • ✓ 240 Memory Channels Across 16 Zones: Full alphanumeric naming for Wing repeater summits, SAR tactical simplex, and inter-agency mutual aid channels.
Audio Management

Garmin GMA 1347 NAV III Audio Panel

Integrated with the Cessna 182T G1000 suite, routing COM1 (ATC/Airband), COM2 (Flight Following), COM3 (TDFM-136B Tactical FM), and intercom channels across the flight crew.

  • ✓ ICS Isolation Modes (Table 6A.3.2): Pilot can isolate to communicate exclusively with ATC while the Mission Observer and Scanner maintain tactical coordination on COM3 with ground teams without interrupting the pilot.
  • ✓ Digital Clearance Recorder: Continuous rolling 2.5-minute digital audio buffer stores recent transmissions. Observer or pilot can press replay to verify complex latitude/longitude coordinates or ATC clearance readbacks.
  • ✓ Split-COM Operational Capability: Pilot transmits on COM1 (121.5 or CTAF) while Observer simultaneously works COM3 (TDFM FM net) without audio cross-talk or bleed-through.
  • ✓ Acoustic RF SNR Degradation: Direction finder audio reproduces authentic distance-attenuated white noise and hiss (elt_noise.wav), teaching crews to recognize distant ELT flutter.
Operational Workflow

Cockpit Coordination & Search Execution

The authentic division of responsibilities during airborne homing.

👨‍✈️

Search Pilot Duties

  • ✓ Fly assigned search altitude (typically 1,000–1,500 ft AGL) and search airspeed (80–90 KIAS).
  • ✓ Execute heading corrections called by the Mission Observer ("Steer right 020 to home").
  • ✓ Maintain outside visual scan, terrain clearance, and ATC communication on COM1.
  • ✓ Manage engine cowl flaps and CHT cooling during low-speed search loitering.
🎧

Mission Observer Duties

  • ✓ Operate DF head: select band (121.5 VHF or 406 MHz), adjust squelch, and monitor spread bars.
  • ✓ Call directional corrections to pilot based on relative bearing pointers and signal strength.
  • ✓ Identify station passage (antenna cone of confusion needle swing and rapid signal dip).
  • ✓ Log coordinates on G1000 MFD and transmit beacon position to Mission Base on TDFM-136B.
