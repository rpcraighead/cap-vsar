CAP 487 · Airborne maneuver deck

Position the aircraft in a cleared exercise block before acquiring control. Manage power manually. Stop other AI pilot controllers before using this deck.

Prototype: heading and indicated-altitude selection. No terrain lookahead, ATC, automatic takeoff, landing or speed control. Confirm the aircraft altimeter setting. Telemetry or envelope faults request a simulator pause.
Also: maintain 4500 feet indicated. One explicit command at a time. Directional turns are not supported yet.
