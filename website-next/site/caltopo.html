ROADMAP

Roadmap · plan only

Base staff on the map they already use.

Some wings work real searches on CalTopo. The plan is for the exercise to meet them there: assignments pushed from the exercise, live aircraft tracks on the CalTopo map, crew marks coming back.

WHAT IS DESIGNED

Staff work the exercise on their own map; the beacon stays hidden.

The integration plan draws one hard line: the relay that talks to CalTopo can never see the hidden beacon. Each sortie gets its own relay, the pattern polygon pushed to CalTopo is the same geometry the crew flies, and a feature drawn by staff in CalTopo comes back attributed to the seat that drew it.

WHERE IT STANDS

Nothing is built yet.

No code, accounts or CalTopo maps exist yet. It waits on a wing that uses CalTopo for exercises and wants to help test it.

Does your wing use CalTopo?

If your wing runs its exercises on CalTopo and would help test this, that is what moves it off the roadmap. A contact address for the project will be added here before the site goes public.

CAP-VSAR is an unofficial training project built by Civil Air Patrol members. It is not an official Civil Air Patrol product and is not endorsed by Civil Air Patrol. "Civil Air Patrol" and "CAP" identify the organization this training is for; no CAP seal, emblem or wing patch is used here. Everything on this site is simulation: it is not for real flight planning, real direction finding or a real search.

Product names are the property of their owners and are used to identify the equipment being simulated.

CalTopo is a product of its owner, named only to identify the mapping service some wings use. CAP-VSAR is not affiliated with or endorsed by CalTopo.
