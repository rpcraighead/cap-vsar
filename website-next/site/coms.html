Unofficial training tool. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Civil Air Patrol.

CAP-VSAR is an unofficial training project built by Civil Air Patrol members. It is not an official Civil Air Patrol product and is not endorsed by Civil Air Patrol. "Civil Air Patrol" and "CAP" identify the organization this training is for; no CAP seal, emblem or wing patch is used here. Everything on this site is simulation: it is not for real flight planning, real direction finding or a real search.

Product names are the property of their owners and are used to identify the equipment being simulated.

Not for operational use.
