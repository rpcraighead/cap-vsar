## B. New illustrations (briefs for GPT Sol / image generation or the builder)

Style tokens for every brief: background charcoal-navy `#0B1220`; accents safety-orange `#FF6A13` (only for the
beacon/ELT and signals), cyan `#27C7D9` (bearing lines, data links), sectional green `#7FBF4D` and deep green
`#3F7A32` (terrain), muted yellow `#E9D45C` (towns/airfields on maps), hairlines `#27344A`; light panels `#F3EFE6`
with ink `#15202B`. Line art with a few flat fills, thin consistent stroke (2 px at 1200 px), no gradients except a
soft orange glow on the beacon; **no text in generated art** (labels are added in HTML/SVG); no CAP seal or emblem,
no manufacturer logos, no real faces. Deliver SVG where possible, else PNG at 2× (2400 px wide) with transparent
background. Fonts for any SVG labels the builder adds: Barlow Condensed (headlines) and JetBrains Mono (chips).

## C. Third-party content check for published images

* Our own web pages and faceplates (Base Radio, Exercise Console, Mission Base, Debrief, Ready Room, Setup,
  Flight Planner, instrument previews): original work. They carry manufacturer and model names (Motorola APX 6500,
  Icom IC-A120, Becker SAR-DF 517, RHOTHETA RT-600, Technisonic TDFM-136B, Garmin GMA 1347) used descriptively, and
  faceplate artwork that follows the manufacturers' manuals in layout. Status **needs-check** for trade dress: keep
  the captions factual ("a training replica of the control head"), do not use manufacturer logos as images, and
  do not claim affiliation. The repository's NOTICE already states the faceplates are original work.
* Map backgrounds: FAA VFR Sectional tiles, public domain; the Leaflet attribution control is visible in S-GUIDE-13
  and S-GUIDE-18. **OK.** Never use the Esri satellite or OpenStreetMap layers in a published capture (licensing
  and attribution terms differ).
* Laminar Research: any capture of the X-Plane cockpit, the stock G1000 screens, or the C172SP-derived panel is
  Laminar artwork. The 2026-10-12 commit `60f5203` removed the Laminar C172 render from the old site and the
  handbook; follow the same line: **remove / do not capture**. The exterior-only shot (N-AIRPLANE-EXT) shows the GPL
  donor exterior, not Laminar art; crop the panel out.
* Air Manager (Sim Innovations): a live screenshot of the MO station would contain the stock G1000 MFD video and
  Air Manager UI chrome. Use the generated layout composite `panels/cap-c182t-mo-station/preview.png`, where the
  stock instrument is a labelled placeholder. **OK (composite only).**
* g1000-sar pages drawn over the Laminar G1000: four display-only screenshots are now on the Airplane page (M1) in a generic drawn bezel. **G1000 display screenshots: [licence] owner decision pending (owner not concerned for now).** The wide cockpit still (`cockpit-wide.webp`) shows the simulator's own bezels and branding and is not used.

  `cap.gov` domain — retake with `example.org` before public use (N-SIGNIN-CLEAN). The Ready Room capture shows
  only roles and callsigns.
* Security: no capture or copy may show LAN addresses, hostnames, the WireGuard setup, access codes, badge codes
  (the printable-badges capture shows full badge codes of a test roster — do not publish it), or repository
  deploy keys. The old `website/deployment.html` lists an internal address; nothing from it is carried over.
